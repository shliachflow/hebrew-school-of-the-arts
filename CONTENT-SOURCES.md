made up" is a fair question and the answer needs to be checkable rather than reassuring.

Three tiers:

- **[V] Verified** — read directly off the client's live site at jewishtroy.com, or otherwise
  confirmed. Safe to publish.
- **[C] Client-supplied, unverified** — from Menachem's copy document. That document was
  AI-generated (confirmed on the 2026-08-13 call), so it is a *draft of intent*, not a record of
  fact. Several items were bracketed placeholders in the original. **Every [C] item needs Menachem
  to confirm before launch.**
- **[W] Written for the site** — connective copy, headings and framing written during the build.
  Contains no factual assertions beyond what [V] or [C] supports.

### Removed during the build

- **Four invented photo captions** — "The whole school, one table", "Hands on, every Sunday",
  "Parents welcome", "Real tools, real trust". All written for the build, and all misleading: the
  photographs are from a single event at a supermarket, not weekly school sessions. Replaced with
  one honest credit line. **Do not caption documentary photographs with marketing claims.**
- Hebrew month names (Elul, Kislev, Adar, Nissan, Sivan) — replaced with holiday names, which are
  both plainer English and more widely understood.
- Insider vocabulary: tefillos, Shabbos, mitzvos, parsha, kashered, Gemara, haftarah, Alef-Bet,
  sufganiyot, "Jewish manhood". The school's core pitch is that unaffiliated families belong, and
  that pitch fails if the homepage assumes the reader already knows the words.
