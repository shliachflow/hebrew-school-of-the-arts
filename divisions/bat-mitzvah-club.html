Division 04 · Girls, ages 9–13

Becoming the woman she's meant to be.

Her own program, her own space, her own mentors. Part learning, part kitchen, part art, and part something harder to put on a website: a room full of girls who are proud of who they are, guided by women they look up to.

What the year holds

Her own room, her own year.

  1. Her own learning

    What it means to grow into a Jewish woman, and the traditions that are specifically hers to carry.

  2. Culinary workshops

    Dessert design and pastry work in the kitchen. This division goes further into technique than any other.

  3. Creative projects and journaling

    Real time in the art studio, plus writing that stays private. Not everything a twelve-year-old makes needs to be handed in.

  4. Bat Mitzvah preparation

    Personal mentorship in the lead-up, worked around your family's date. Call to talk through your daughter's timeline.

Both teen divisions cover the same core learning, each in the way that reaches them best. See how a Sunday runs.
