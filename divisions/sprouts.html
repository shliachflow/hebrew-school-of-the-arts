Division 01 · Ages 4–5

Their first taste of everything.

The beginning of a Jewish life, and it should be delicious. Sprouts runs on songs, snacks, mess and repetition. They will know their Hebrew letters, the holidays, and the smell of a Friday kitchen before they can write their own name.

What the year holds

Built for three, four and five year olds.

  1. First Hebrew letters

    Through song, movement and play. There are no worksheets at this age, and there is no pressure to read.

  2. Simple kitchen projects

    Mixing, rolling, decorating. Small hands, real ingredients, and everything they make goes home with them.

  3. Art and sensory stations

    Paint, dough and texture, plus story time. The art room is a big part of why the little ones ask to come back.

  4. Their first holidays

    Celebrations built for the age group, so the holidays arrive as something they did rather than something they were told about.

Sprouts follows the same Sunday rhythm as the rest of the school, adapted for the age: more movement, more snack, and shorter stretches of sitting. See how a Sunday runs.
