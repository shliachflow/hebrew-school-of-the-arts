Division 03 · Boys, ages 9–13

Real fire. Real knives. Real responsibility.

The boys' division is built on something eleven-year-old boys almost never get: actual trust. They run their own stations in a working kitchen, with real equipment and real heat. They take on real Torah learning. And they reach their Bar Mitzvah having done a great deal more than memorize a reading.

What the year holds

Trusted with the real thing.

  1. The kitchen, theirs to run

    Their own stations, real equipment, real heat. They measure, they knead, they burn a batch and make it again.

  2. Learning that respects them

    Torah, an introduction to Talmud, and room for the questions boys this age actually ask rather than the ones adults expect.

  3. Tefillin, and growing up

    The practical side of becoming Bar Mitzvah, and an honest conversation about what it means to grow up Jewish.

  4. Bar Mitzvah preparation

    One-on-one coaching, worked around your family's date. Call Rabbi Caytak to talk through your son's timeline.
