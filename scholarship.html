Scholarship Assistance

No child is turned away.

Thanks to the Long Lake Plaza Fund and the support of H.E.R Management, families can apply for partial or full assistance with tuition. Apply before you register.

  • ConfidentialApplications are private. No proof of income and no financial statements are required.
  • Partial or fullTell us what you are able to contribute toward tuition this year, or request full assistance.
  • $100 still appliesThe registration fee per child is not covered by scholarship assistance.
  • AttendanceScholarship families commit to consistent Sunday attendance. Illness and family emergencies are of course understood.

Jewish education should be available to every Jewish child, regardless of what a family can afford.
