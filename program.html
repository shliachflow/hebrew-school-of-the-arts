The Program

What your child actually learns.

Hebrew reading taught by level, a working kitchen, a dedicated art studio, and a full Jewish curriculum. Here is all of it, in one place.

Hebrew Reading

Ten levels. They know exactly which one they're on.

We teach reading with Aleph Champ, which works the way karate belts do. Every child starts where they actually are and moves up one color at a time. A beginner is never behind, an advanced reader is never bored, and progress is something a seven-year-old can see.

Beyond the Classroom

A real kitchen. Not a hot plate in a classroom.

Every division rotates through the kitchen every month. They measure, they knead, they burn a batch and make it again.

  • Rosh HashanahChallah, braided six different ways
  • HanukkahLatkes and jelly doughnuts, from scratch
  • PurimA hamantaschen production line
  • PassoverMatzah, eighteen minutes, hands covered in flour
  • ShavuotCheesecake, obviously

Whatever they make, they take home.

The Art Room

The “Arts” was never decoration.

There has been a dedicated art teacher here since the beginning, and that room is the reason a lot of kids show up. Mosaics, painting, sculpture, ceramics, mixed media — always tied to what they're learning.

They don't take home a worksheet. They take home an heirloom.

A menorah they designed themselves. A seder plate their family will use for twenty years.

The Substance

Fun is the method. This is the goal.

What your child should be able to do by the end of the year.

  1. Read Hebrew

    Actual letters, actual words, actual prayers, at their own level and without pressure.

  2. Know the holidays

    Not just when they fall. What they mean, why we do what we do, and how to run one at home.

  3. Own a piece of the story

    Abraham, Joseph, the Maccabees, Esther. Our history as their inheritance rather than a textbook unit.

  4. Do it themselves

    Say a blessing. Braid a challah. Light a menorah. Lead the Four Questions at the seder.

  5. Be proud out loud

    The one we care about most. In a world that gives Jewish kids plenty of reasons to be quiet, we are raising kids who aren't.

The Curriculum

Six things we teach, every year.

  1. Hebrew

    Students learn to read and write Hebrew and gain confidence with the siddur, the prayer book. We use Aleph Champ so that every child reads Hebrew by the end of their time here.

  2. Jewish values

    Judaism offers values that give a person the tools to get the most out of life. Our students explore the ones that will guide them into adulthood.

  3. Traditions, holidays and Shabbat

    By living the traditions rather than reading about them, children gain a deeper understanding and a real affection for them.

  4. Torah

    Students study the messages of the Torah and the practical lessons in them that apply to daily life.

  5. Israel

    Children learn Israel's history and geography, and build a connection to it.

  6. Jewish history

    An appreciation of the history and culture that has been carried and preserved across the generations.
